Biorenewables Development Centre Facilities & Equipment

Open-access process development, analytical and scale-up facilities for bio-based innovation.

The BDC helps companies convert plants, microbes and biowastes into profitable biorenewable products, bridging the gap between academia and industry. Our technical specialists provide open-access facilities to develop, scale-up and test your processes.

Process development & scale-up
Fermentation up to 30 L
Pre-processing & biomass handling
Separation & downstream processing
Analytical & molecular platforms
Access to specialist scientific expertise
