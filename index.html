Bridging the gap between academia and industry.

The Biorenewables Development Centre (BDC) supports companies in converting plants, microbes, and biowastes into profitable, sustainable products.

Our technical specialists manage a comprehensive suite of equipment, enabling process development from bench scale through to pilot scale. Whether optimising a novel extraction, scaling up a fermentation process, or characterising complex organic compounds, our facilities are designed to de-risk innovation.

  • Open-access technical facilities
  • Pilot-scale process equipment
